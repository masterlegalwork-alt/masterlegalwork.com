Client & Case Portal

A proposed secure workspace for client records, court matters, case documents, hearings, case correspondence and advocate-reviewed AI assistance.

Secure portal is not activated. Client logins, document uploads, matter records and AI review of case files are not yet available on this page. No confidential case information is collected here. Access will be enabled only after authentication, privacy and security controls are deployed and tested.

Client profile

Contact records, verified identity, authorised representatives and separate case references.

Complete case file

One confidential workspace per matter, with parties, court, number, status, dates and internal notes.

Document register

Pleadings, affidavits, evidence, orders, notices, scanned papers and version history, each linked to its originating matter.

Hearings & actions

Court dates, tasks, filing deadlines, responsible team member, and status updates after verification.

Accounts & receipts

Professional fees, expenses, invoices, payment references and outstanding balances, with restricted visibility.

AI case assistant

Proposed private, advocate-controlled summaries, chronologies and document comparisons with source references. No automatic case advice to clients.

Confidentiality and access

Every matter must be protected by server-side authentication and access checks. Clients should see only records explicitly released to them, not internal notes, other clients' documents or preliminary AI work. All uploads will require authorisation, malware screening and restricted storage. Google or other social sign-in must never by itself grant access to a legal matter.
