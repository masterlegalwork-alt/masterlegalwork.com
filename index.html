About

A practice built on preparation and teamwork

Master Legal Work is the chamber of Advocate Gagandeep Goel at the District & Sessions Court, Sector 43, Chandigarh. Gagandeep Goel was enrolled with the Bar Council of Punjab & Haryana in 2007 (Enrolment No. P/1983/2007) and has about 19 years of practice before courts and tribunals in Chandigarh.

Teamwork is central to how the chamber works. Associates, clerks and counsel coordinate research, drafting and hearing preparation together, so every matter is prepared with care before it reaches the court.
