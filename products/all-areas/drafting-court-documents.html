Drafting and formatting of court documents

Drafting, or formatting to the court's filing requirements, of a petition, application, reply or written statement that you specify, on the facts and documents you provide.

What is included

  1. Draft prepared on your facts and documents
  2. Formatting, indexing and page marking to the filing court's requirements
  3. One round of revisions
Format
DOCX and PDF
Delivery
By email after payment and receipt of your documents; turnaround confirmed in writing before payment
