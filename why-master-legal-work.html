The chambers

How the chambers work

A structured, transparent and timely way of handling each matter: what you can expect from the first booking to the closing note. A description of working methods, not a promise about any outcome.

What clients receive

Working methods followed on every matter, with optional services marked as such.

  • Structured intake before consultation

    A short client information form and your documents are taken before the meeting, so the consultation is spent on the questions that matter.

  • Written minutes

    A written note of each consultation and meeting: what was discussed, the course agreed and what you need to provide.

  • Order sharing with plain-language notes

    A copy of every order with the next date, free of charge on every matter, with a short note in plain language on what the order means and what comes next.

  • Client Portal and case Drive folder

    An optional paid service: a private Google Drive case folder holding your complete case record, order sheets shared within 24 hours of availability, and the relevant judgments on the issues in your matter.

  • Hearing alerts on your calendar

    With the Client Portal, hearing dates and reminders are added to your own Google Calendar for the life of the matter.

  • Transparent, stage-wise process

    Each stage of the matter is explained to you before it begins.

  • Free model formats and court directory

    Model formats, checklists, guides, cause-list and VC links, and a directory of courts and police stations, free to use without signing up.

The client journey

Six steps, each explained to you before it begins.

  1. IntakeBooking, client information form and your documents.
  2. ConsultationIn person, by telephone, on WhatsApp or by video call.
  3. Strategy noteA written note of the options, the forum and the steps ahead.
  4. FilingDrafting, your approval of the draft, and filing.
  5. UpdatesEvery order and next date, with a plain-language note.
  6. ClosureA closing note and the return of your documents and record.
