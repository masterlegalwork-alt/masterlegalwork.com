How we work on your matter

Four steps from your first conversation to a clear handover. Your own matter page shows which step you are at.

01 Agree the plan

Tell us what you need. We confirm the work, fee and first delivery date.

02 Review the work

You receive the agreed draft or advice. We explain what needs your approval.

03 See the progress

We share proof of filing or dispatch when included. Each update tells you who acts next.

04 Get your handover

You receive the agreed records, a plain-language summary and any remaining dates.

What every update tells you

Done

Your application was filed. The filing receipt is attached.

Waiting

The registry is checking the papers. A hearing date is pending.

Next update

We will check on Friday and update you even if the date is still pending.

Example for a property dispute. Court dates depend on the forum. Statutory deadlines and court directions take priority.
