What we collect. Your name, email address, mobile number and login details. We also keep the details of your matters, hearing dates and documents that our office records for you, and anything you send through the enquiry or consultation forms.

Why we collect it. To respond to your enquiry, to provide legal services to you, to keep you informed about your matters and hearing dates, and to fix consultations.

Who can see it. Your matter records can be seen only by you, after logging in, and by our office. We do not sell your data. It is shared only as needed to provide legal services or as required by law.
