Prepare before you open the portal

  1. Identify the court or commission and keep the case type, number and year ready.
  2. Check that you have selected the correct district, bench or commission.
  3. Read the latest order as well as the displayed status; save the official document for your record.
  4. Check the hearing date against the applicable cause list and any later order.

Enter case details, passwords, OTPs and payment information only on the official portal. The resource search here finds links and does not search a court database.

Questions litigants commonly ask

How do I check my next hearing date?

Open the official case-status service for your court, locate the correct case, and read the latest case history or order. Then check the cause list for the relevant date. A case-status entry, a cause-list entry and the order serve different purposes; confirm any discrepancy with your advocate or the court registry.

What is a CNR number and where do I enter it?

A CNR is the 16-character alphanumeric case identifier used by eCourts. Enter it without spaces or hyphens in the official CNR search and complete that portal's CAPTCHA. If you do not know it, use the case-number, party-name or other available search option.

How do I find a case in Mohali?

Use the SAS Nagar district court website or select Punjab and SAS Nagar in the district-court case-status service. Mohali is commonly searched under SAS Nagar. Keep the court, case type, number and year available.

Is a cause list the same as a hearing order?

No. A cause list identifies matters listed before a court for a date, often with a bench and item number. The hearing order records the directions actually passed. Check the order and any supplementary or revised cause list.

Where can I check a consumer complaint?

Use the official e-Jagriti Case History/Status service. Select the correct commission and enter the supported case or filing reference. The government platform also provides filing and help services.

Can I obtain an order from this website?

This directory links you to the official court or tribunal service; it does not retrieve your case record. Search the official portal for an available order. If a certified copy is required, follow the court's copying procedure.

Are my favourites shared with the chambers?

No. Favourite resource links are stored only in this browser on this device. This feature stores resource identifiers, not your CNR, party names, passwords or case documents.

What if an official portal does not open?

Try the court's official homepage or the related national eCourts portal. Government services can require JavaScript, a CAPTCHA or sign-in and can be temporarily unavailable. For an urgent listing or deadline, confirm with the registry or your advocate.
